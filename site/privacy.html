Privacy notice

Last updated: 20 October 2026

The short version

GridHive runs on your own computer. It has no account system and no analytics, and it sends nothing about you or your work to its authors.

Early-access waitlist

If you join the waitlist on gridhive.dev, we store your email address and the date you signed up in our database (Neon, hosted in the EU or US) to send you one email when accounts and phone access from anywhere are ready. We do not share it or use it for anything else. To be removed, open an issue on GitHub or reply to that email, and we delete it.

What stays on your computer

  • Your board (agents, layout, projects, jobs, actions, settings) is stored in the .gridhive folder in your home directory (.agentarmy on installs from before the rename).
  • The local server only listens on 127.0.0.1, protected by a random access token.
  • Your Claude login stays with Claude Code. GridHive never reads or stores your password or tokens. To show usage, it reads token counts, never message content, from Claude Code's local logs.

When data leaves your computer

  • Claude Code talks to Anthropic as it always does, under your own Claude account and Anthropic's terms and privacy policy.
  • Notifications you turn on (Slack, Discord or ntfy) send short messages, such as "Ada needs you", to the service you configure.
  • Updates: the Windows app checks GitHub Releases for new versions. GitHub sees a normal download request.
  • Browser tiles load the websites you or your agents open, like any browser.

This website

This site is a static page hosted by Vercel. It sets no cookies and uses no tracking. Vercel may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security and operations; see Vercel's privacy policy. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
